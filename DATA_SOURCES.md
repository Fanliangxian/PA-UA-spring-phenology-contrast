# Data sources

This archive accompanies the matched urban-area (UA) and protected-area (PA) spring-phenology study. The frozen sample contains 18,862 pairs. Phenological summaries cover 2003–2024; vegetation eligibility and long-term tree cover use 2001–2024.

Product identifiers below specify the inputs used in the study. The archive provides author-generated sampling records, derived statistics and code; it does not mirror the full upstream datasets. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for source-specific terms and [ASSET_ACCESS.md](ASSET_ACCESS.md) for reconstruction requirements.

## Products and their roles

| Source and version                                             | Study use                                                                                                                                 | Official access and citation                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| MODIS MCD12Q2 Collection 6.1; `MODIS/061/MCD12Q2`              | Greenup dates, cycle structure and quality flags for 2003–2024; common-support counts, paired differences and temporal summaries          | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MCD12Q2); [MCD12Q2.061 DOI](https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MCD12Q2.061)            |
| MODIS MCD12Q1 Collection 6.1; `MODIS/061/MCD12Q1`              | Land-cover eligibility over 2001–2024, IGBP/PFT vegetation tiers and the native sampling grid                                             | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MCD12Q1); [MCD12Q1.061 DOI](https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MCD12Q1.061)            |
| MODIS MOD44B Collection 6.1; `MODIS/061/MOD44B`                | Annual tree-cover fraction, aggregated to the sampling grid before the 2001–2024 median; vegetation eligibility and matching              | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MOD44B)                                                                           |
| MERIT DEM v1.0.3; `MERIT/DEM/v1_0_3`                           | Elevation and slope matching covariates. Slope was computed from the DEM; both variables were averaged onto the sampling grid             | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MERIT_DEM_v1_0_3); [Yamazaki et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1002/2017GL072874)           |
| WorldClim V1 bioclimatic variables; `WORLDCLIM/V1/BIO`         | Mean annual temperature from `bio01 × 0.1`; annual precipitation from `bio12`; `logMAP = ln(max(MAP, 1))`. These were matching covariates | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/WORLDCLIM_V1_BIO); [Hijmans et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1002/joc.1276)                |
| WorldClim V1 monthly climatology; `WORLDCLIM/V1/MONTHLY`       | Monthly temperature and precipitation normals, combined with mean annual temperature, for the study's approximate major climate groups    | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/WORLDCLIM_V1_MONTHLY)                                                                       |
| RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017; `RESOLVE/ECOREGIONS/2017`             | Ecoregion and biome matching strata; realm codes and corresponding geographic summaries                                                   | [Earth Engine catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/RESOLVE_ECOREGIONS_2017); [Dinerstein et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014) |
| Global Urban Boundaries, year 2000 (GUB2000)                   | Urban sampling domain, using the study's archived 1 km buffered boundary                                                                  | [Li et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ab9be3); [producer-associated data page](https://www.scl.hku.hk/data)                                                     |
| World Database on Protected Areas (WDPA), archived study input | PA sampling domain, restricted to protected areas established before 2001                                                                 | [Protected Planet](https://www.protectedplanet.net/); [source terms](https://www.protectedplanet.net/en/legal)                                                                 |
| Natural Earth low-resolution basemap                           | Geographic context in maps; it does not determine sampling eligibility                                                                    | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/); [terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)                                                       |

WorldClim BIO and MONTHLY have distinct roles: the annual matching covariates were extracted from BIO, while MONTHLY supported climate grouping. The native MCD12Q1 sampling grid used for domain-overlap calculations is identified separately from the MCD12Q2 phenology product.
